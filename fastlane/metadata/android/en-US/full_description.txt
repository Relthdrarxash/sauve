Sauve is an open-source first aid and vital emergency app, designed to guide you quickly and accurately when every second counts.

Free, open-source (GPL-3.0), ad-free, account-free, and 100% offline: it does not even ask for Internet permission. It works instantly everywhere, even in dead zones, undergrounds, or remote outdoor trails.

THE 4 VITAL EMERGENCIES STEP BY STEP
• Cardiac Arrest: Express responsiveness and breathing diagnosis, direct emergency dispatch (112 / 911), 110 bpm haptic metronome vibrating right in your pocket so you can keep both hands and eyes on the victim, vocal compression count guidance (continuous or 30:2 ratio), rescuer rotation reminder every 2 minutes, and AED operation guide.
• Severe Choking: Immediate detection of partial vs total airway obstruction, guided 5 back blows / 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver), infant Mofenson variant, and one-tap switch to CPR in case of unconsciousness.
• Severe Bleeding: Immediate direct pressure, pressure points, emergency tourniquet application with automatic timestamp logging and medical handover instructions.
• Unconsciousness / Recovery Position: Airway check, illustrated step-by-step Recovery Position (PLS), and continuous breathing monitoring.

DIRECT EMERGENCY CALLS
Quick one-tap access to emergency numbers adapted to European and French frameworks (15 SAMU, 18 Fire, 112 European Emergency, 114 SMS for deaf and hard-of-hearing).

EVIDENCE-BASED GUIDELINES
All protocols strictly adhere to international recommendations from the European Resuscitation Council (ERC 2021) and certified first aid frameworks (PSC1).

ZERO DATA COLLECTION
Your phone is your personal intervention tool. No tracking, no user account, no analytics. Sauve does not replace certified first aid training, but gives you clear guidance when the unexpected occurs.
