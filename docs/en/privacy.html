Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

In short

Sauve collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no tracker, no advertising and no analytics. Whatever you enter or the app produces stays on your phone.

Internet

The app does not declare the Internet permission in its Android manifest. It is technically unable to send or receive data over the network.

Permissions requested

  • Vibration: for the metronome and alerts.
  • Notifications: only for an optional reminder to review the gestures, off by default. You can refuse this permission without losing any emergency feature.

Calls and text messages

Sauve does not place calls itself. It opens the Phone app with the emergency number already filled in, and you start the call. For 114 it opens your messaging app. What happens next is handled by those apps.

Voice

Reading aloud uses the speech engine installed on your phone. Sauve only gives it the text to read and does not control how it works.

Data kept on the phone

The app keeps its settings on the device, for example whether the reminder is on. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.
